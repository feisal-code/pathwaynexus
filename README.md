## 📖 The Problem
Skilled displaced people arrive with proven experience—engineers, nurses, finance professionals, teachers—yet often wait years for work that matches their skills. The barriers are rarely ability. They are **unrecognized credentials, lack of local experience, and missing professional networks.**

Millions hold qualifications that are not recognized locally, and networks—not qualifications—often decide who gets hired.

## 💡 The Solution
**Pathway Nexus is not just another job board.** It is a connected journey that turns existing experience into clear career pathways through skills analysis, opportunity matching, mentorship, and actionable next steps.

### The 5-Step Journey
1. **Profile** — Build a structured professional profile.
2. **Skills Passport** — Map transferable skills to local market needs.
3. **Opportunity Match** — Match proven skills with real local opportunities.
4. **Mentor Match** — Connect with industry professionals who can vouch for their work (e.g., Amina K., Senior Finance Manager).
5. **Local Experience** — Bridge the gap through internships, job shadowing, volunteering, and credential recognition support.

## ✨ Key Features

### For Displaced Talent
- **Instant Demo:** Try the platform without signing up.
- **Explainable Matching:** See exactly why a job or mentor matches your skills (e.g., 92% Match).
- **Local Experience Pathways:** Access internships, shadowing, and credential recognition support.
- **Personalized Next Steps:** Clear, actionable progress tracking.

### For Employers, NGOs & Partners
- **Anonymized Talent Search:** Filter candidates by skill, field, language, and location.
- **Privacy-First Outreach:** Express interest in a candidate without seeing their legal status or personal history.
- **Ecosystem Building:** Connecting NGOs, universities, and employers into one coordinated pathway.

---

## 🔒 Built on Trust & Privacy
Trust is our foundation. Dealing with vulnerable populations requires strict ethical boundaries:
- **Privacy First:** We ask *only* for professional information. Never legal status, ID numbers, or personal history. Profiles stay on the user's device.
- **Responsible AI:** AI results are clearly labeled as assessments and recommendations, *not* credential verification.
- **Ethical Demo:** The prototype uses fictional demo opportunities, mentors, and profiles (the hackathon team is real!).

## 🌍 SDG Alignment
Pathway Nexus was built directly against three Sustainable Development Goals:
- **SDG 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth):** Moving skilled refugees from underemployment into meaningful work.
- **SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities):** Removing the credential-recognition and network barriers that keep displaced talent out of the local labor market.
- **SDG 17 (Partnerships for the Goals):** Connecting employers, NGOs, universities, and refugee-led organizations into one coordinated pathway.
